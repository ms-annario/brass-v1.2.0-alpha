# Contingency Plan CP-2026-1102: Chain-Reaction Accident on Bedok North Road

**Requested by:** Bus Operations Control (fictional), on advice from the Traffic Police
**Prepared for:** Network Operations & Bus Services
**Date issued:** 02/11/2026
**Effective period:** 02/11/2026 06:00 to 08/11/2026 23:59 (temporary, estimated)
**Project name in BRASS:** Emergency Incident: Chain-Reaction Accident along Bedok North Road
**Plan status:** Activated

> Mock-up for testing document upload in BRASS. The incident, team and contacts are fictional; the roads, stops and buses are the ones on the BRASS map.

## 1. Background
A serious chain-reaction accident involving more than 10 vehicles has happened on Bedok North Road. The Traffic Police have blocked the affected road section while emergency teams manage the scene. They expect the situation to improve within about one week.

Bus services that normally use this stretch can no longer run their usual routes, and the operations team must respond immediately.

## 2. Summary
- Bedok North Road is shut to all traffic from Blk 434 to Blk 875 (section 3), with an incident zone kept clear for emergency vehicles (section 4).
- Bus service 36 runs through this stretch on the BRASS map. It will be diverted around it (section 5).
- Three stops on the blocked stretch will not be served. Riders board at the nearest stops service 36 still serves: Tampines Sports Complex (75016) and Opp Blk 234 (75019) (section 6).
- The plan stays in force until the Traffic Police reopen the road, expected by 08/11/2026.

## 3. Road closure
- **Road:** Bedok North Road, between Blk 434 (75017) and Blk 875 (75018).
- **Closure:** both directions, full closure, 24 hours a day, until the Traffic Police reopen the road.
- **Reason:** chain-reaction accident involving more than 10 vehicles; Traffic Police investigation and vehicle recovery.

## 4. Incident zone
- **Incident zone:** 150 m around Blk 434 (75017), the crash site. Kept clear for police, ambulances, fire engines and recovery cranes; no buses may enter.

## 5. Affected services
| Bus | Change |
|-----|--------|
| 36 | Divert around the blocked section of Bedok North Road. Skip Blk 434 (75017) and Blk 875 (75018). |

- **Fallback:** if the diversion is too slow or congested, curtail service 36 on its own route with the "Curtail" option in BRASS, ending each section at the nearest clear stop or interchange on either side of the blockage.
- **Any other service** found on the blocked section or inside the incident zone in BRASS is diverted the same way.

## 7. Response timeline
| When | Action | Owner |
|------|--------|-------|
| First 30 minutes | Hold buses away from the scene; confirm the blocked section with the Traffic Police | Bus Operations Control |
| First 2 hours | Draw the closure and incident zone in BRASS; divert service 36; mark the three stops not served | Network Planning |
| First 2 hours | Post notices at the stops not served and on the operator app | Customer Communications |

| Days 2 to 7 | Daily check with the Traffic Police; keep the plan or switch to the fallback | Duty Manager |

## 8. Roles and responsibilities
| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| Duty Manager | Activates and stands down this plan; single contact for the Traffic Police |
| Bus Operations Control | Holds, diverts and adds buses; watches journey times |
| Network Planning | Keeps the BRASS project up to date and exports the change report and diversion poster |
| Customer Communications | Notices at stops, operator app and hotline messages |

## 9. Communication
1. Export the diversion poster from this BRASS project and put it up at the three stops not served and at Tampines Sports Complex (75016) and Opp Blk 234 (75019).
2. Announce the diversion on the operator app and at Downtown Int within 2 hours.
3. Update the notices whenever the Traffic Police change the expected reopening date.

## 10. Triggers and escalation
| Trigger | Response |
|---------|----------|
| Road still shut after 08/11/2026 | Extend the project end date in BRASS and re-issue the notices |
| Diversion adds more than 15 minutes to a journey | Switch service 36 to the fallback (curtail) |
| One direction reopens first | Reopen that direction in BRASS and restore the stops it serves |
| A second incident nearby | Draw it in BRASS as a further affected area and review every service through it |

## 11. Stand-down
When the Traffic Police reopen Bedok North Road:
1. Remove the closure and the incident zone in BRASS.
2. Undo the diversion of service 36 and serve its stops again.
3. Export the change report for the record and take the notices down.

## 12. Required map updates
1. Add a road closure on Bedok North Road between 75017 and 75018 for the effective period.
2. Add the incident zone of 150 m around 75017.
3. Mark stops 75017, 75018 and 75146 as not served for the period.
4. Re-route bus service 36 around the blocked section and the incident zone.
5. Export the change report and the diversion poster.
