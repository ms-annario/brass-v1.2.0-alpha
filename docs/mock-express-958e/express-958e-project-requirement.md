# Project Requirement RQ-2027-0423: New Express Bus Service 958E, Bedok – Jurong East

**Requested by:** Land Transport Authority (LTA), staff commute following the office move to Jurong East
**Prepared for:** Network Operations & Bus Services
**Date issued:** 20/11/2026
**Effective from:** 23/04/2027 06:30 (permanent)
**Project name in BRASS:** New Express Bus Service 958E: Bedok – Jurong East

> Proposal for testing in BRASS. The service number 958E, the timetable and the contacts are made up for this mock-up; the roads, stops and interchanges are the ones on the BRASS map.

## 1. Background
LTA is moving its office from Hampshire Road to Jurong East. More than 1,000 staff live in eastern Singapore and would face a long journey to the new office, changing between several bus services or MRT lines.

LTA is considering a direct express bus between Bedok and Jurong East to give them a faster, one-seat commute.

## 2. Summary
A new express service will run from Bedok North Interchange to Jurong East Interchange from Friday 23 April 2027. It picks up at four stops in Bedok North, runs non-stop across the island, and sets down at four stops in Jurong East, ending at Jurong East Interchange beside Jurong East MRT station.

- **Assumed route:** Bedok to Jurong East (morning direction).
- **Assumption:** the new LTA office is within walking distance of Jurong East Interchange.

## 3. Objectives
1. A direct, one-seat ride from Bedok to Jurong East, with no transfers.
2. A journey of about 50 to 60 minutes in the morning peak.
3. Enough seats for more than 1,000 staff between 6.30am and 8.45am on working days.
4. No change to any existing bus service.

## 4. New bus service 958E (express)
New bus service 958E runs from Bedok North Int to Jurong East Int, stopping at these stops in this order:

| Stop | Code | Use |
|------|------|-----|
| Bedok North Int | 84001 | Board |
| Blk 193 | 84017 | Board |
| Bef Bedok North Ave 5 | 84049 | Board |
| Aft Bedok North Ave 3 | 84019 | Board |
| Aft Jurong East Ave 6 | 27029 | Alight |
| Aft Jurong East Ave 3 | 27031 | Alight |
| Aft Jurong East Ave 1 | 27032 | Alight |
| Jurong East Int | 27001 | Alight |

- **Express section:** non-stop from Aft Bedok North Ave 3 to Aft Jurong East Ave 6, by the fastest roads.
- **Length:** about 23 km end to end.
- **Boarding and alighting:** board only at the four Bedok stops; alight only at the four Jurong East stops.

| Item | Requirement |

## 6. Planning checks
1. Check every road segment on the route for double-deck limits: height clearance, axle weight, lane width and turning radius. Mark each road segment constraint as **Accept** or **Not Accept**; if one is not accepted, re-route service 958E around that segment and keep all eight stops.
2. Make sure the route avoids roads that other projects block at the same time.
3. Confirm bus bays at Bedok North Int and layover space at Jurong East Int for 10 buses.
4. Verify kerbside access at each stop with the **Google Street View** link in the bus stop and bus route info panels.
5. Confirm the route has no gaps, and that every stop takes double-deck buses.

## 7. Out of scope
- Evening return trips from Jurong East to Bedok. They will follow in a separate requirement once the morning route is confirmed.

## 8. Public notice
Put up the launch poster (`express-958e-poster.pdf`) at the eight stops, at Bedok North and Jurong East interchanges, and at Bedok and Jurong East MRT stations from 09/04/2027, two weeks before the first day of service.

## 9. Required map updates
1. Add new bus service 958E with stops 84001, 84017, 84049, 84019, 27029, 27031, 27032 and 27001 in order.
2. Set service 958E to run Monday to Friday from 06:30 to 08:45 with 10 double-deck buses.
3. Apply the project as a permanent change from 23/04/2027 06:30.

## 10. Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Service 958E shows on the map as one continuous route from Bedok North Int to Jurong East Int, through all eight stops in order.
- [ ] No existing bus service is changed.
- [ ] Every road segment constraint on the route is accepted, or the route is re-routed around it.
- [ ] The change report and the launch poster list service 958E as a new service.
