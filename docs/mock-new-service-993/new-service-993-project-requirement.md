# Project Requirement RQ-2026-1120: New Bus Service 993, Tengah – Jurong East

**Effective from:** 04/01/2027 05:30 (permanent)
**Project name in BRASS:** New Bus Service 993: Tengah – Jurong East

> Mock-up for testing document upload in BRASS. The service number, team and contacts are fictional; the roads, stops and interchanges are the ones on the BRASS map.

## 1. Summary
A new bus service 993 will link Tengah Interchange with Jurong East Interchange from Monday 4 January 2027. New flats in Tengah have no direct bus to Jurong East today, so residents change buses or walk to the train. Service 993 runs through the middle of the Tengah estate, along Bukit Batok Avenue 6 and Jurong East Street 26, to Jurong East Interchange, beside Jurong East MRT station (North-South and East-West Lines).

## 2. Objectives
1. A direct, one-seat ride from Tengah to Jurong East in about 22 minutes.
2. A stop within a 5-minute walk of the new Tengah blocks along Tengah Avenue 2, Tengah Street 66 and Tengah Street 76.
3. Train connections at both ends: Tengah MRT at Tengah Interchange and Jurong East MRT at Jurong East Interchange.
4. No change to any existing bus service.

## 5. Planning checks
1. Check every road segment on the route for double-deck limits: height clearance, axle weight, lane width and turning radius. Mark each road segment constraint as **Accept** or **Not Accept**; if one is not accepted, re-route service 993 around that segment and keep all ten stops.
2. Confirm bus bays and layover space at Tengah Int and Jurong East Int for 6 buses.
3. Verify kerbside access at each stop with the **Google Street View** link in the bus stop and bus route info panels.
4. Confirm the route has no gaps, and that every stop takes double-deck buses.

## 6. Public notice
Put up the launch poster (`new-service-993-poster.pdf`) at all ten stops, at both interchanges and at Tengah and Jurong East MRT stations from 21/12/2026, two weeks before the first day of service.

## 7. Required map updates
1. Add new bus service 993 with stops 42001, 42004, 42125, 42096, 28054, 27129, 27150, 27073, 27008 and 27001 in order.
2. Set service 993 to run daily from 05:30 to 23:45 with 6 double-deck buses.
3. Apply the project as a permanent change from 04/01/2027 05:30.

## 8. Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Service 993 shows on the map as one continuous route from Tengah Int to Jurong East Int, through all ten stops in order.
- [ ] No existing bus service is changed.
- [ ] Every road segment constraint on the route is accepted, or the route is re-routed around it.
- [ ] The change report and the launch poster list service 993 as a new service.
